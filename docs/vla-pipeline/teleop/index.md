# Guide the arm with a headset

Teleoperation means a person controls the robot remotely. Here, the Meta Quest tracks your hand, the workstation turns that movement into arm commands, and cameras show you the work area. The same program records images and movements for later model training.

The lab station has two arms. Validate rig A first, then rig B. Follow these pages:

1. [Install the software](../install.md) on the new workstation.
2. [Configure and start](setup.md) the arm, headset, and cameras.
3. [Record your first demonstration](operation.md).
4. [Prepare recordings](datasets.md) when you are ready to use them for training.

The launch script also moves the arm to its home position. Read the movement notice before starting it.
