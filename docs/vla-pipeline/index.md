# VLA Pipeline

A tabletop station where an operator uses VR headsets to control two xArm 7 robots. Wrist and scene cameras show the grippers and work area. It uses the [shared workstation](../getting-started/hardware.md).

**Goal:** assemble and identify both rigs, arrange clear camera views, and have an operator check the connections and stop controls.

!!! info "What you need"
    **Core equipment:** 2 xArm 7 robots with control boxes and grippers · 2 Vention stands · 2 D405 wrist cameras · 3 D415 scene cameras · 2 Quest 3 headsets with 4 controllers · work table, tray, and task tubs.

    **Open items:** scene-camera stand model, a connector check on the pending camera cables, and print settings for the gripper fingers. [Details](../getting-started/sources.md#open-items)
